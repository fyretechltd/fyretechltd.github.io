# Privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers fyretech.co.uk. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page.

## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts. Nothing is loaded from other sites.

## Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as set out in GitHub's privacy statement.

## Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We do not share them, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email [contact@fyretech.co.uk](mailto:contact@fyretech.co.uk).

You can also contact the ICO.
