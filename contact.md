# Contact

The best way to reach us is by email at **[contact@fyretech.co.uk](mailto:contact@fyretech.co.uk)**.

## What to contact us about

- **General questions** about Fyre Tech Ltd or our work
- **App support.** Each app has its own support page, linked from the app's page. Start there, as some apps may have a separate support address.
- **Business enquiries**, such as partnerships, press or working together
- **Privacy requests.** Ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## What to include

To help us reply quickly, please tell us:

- which app or project your message is about
- the device and version you're using, if it's about an app
- what happened, and what you expected to happen
